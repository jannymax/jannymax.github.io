隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

更新日期 / Updated: 2026-11-24

适用范围 / Scope

本政策说明KikiDub 1.0.13的隐私实践，不包含尚未发布的功能。KikiDub不要求注册账号，不含广告或第三方分析追踪SDK。

This policy describes KikiDub version 1.0.13, excluding unreleased features. KikiDub does not require an account and does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

1. 录音与作品 / Recordings and works

配音需要麦克风权限。录音、作品和练习记录默认保存在设备本地，KikiDub不会自动将其上传到我们的服务器。家长主动保存视频、导出作品备份或分享资料时，相关文件会交给家长选择的应用、设备或存储服务；这些服务按各自政策处理文件。

Microphone permission is needed for dubbing. Recordings, works and practice records are stored on your device; KikiDub does not automatically upload them to our servers. When a parent exports a video, works backup or learning material, the selected app, device or storage service receives the file and handles it under its own policies.

2. 联网内容 / Online content

应用通过Cloudflare课程内容服务下载目录、教学媒体及家长资料，通过GitHub下载每日内容和月报封面，通过Apple查询公开版本信息。课程下载可能使用备用中转线路。这些请求不包含孩子的录音或作品，也不附加KikiDub创建的用户或设备跟踪标识。服务提供方可能处理IP地址、请求时间、文件路径等连接信息以完成内容传输和安全防护。

The app downloads catalogs, lesson media and parent materials from its Cloudflare content service, daily content and album covers from GitHub, and public version information from Apple. Course downloads may use a fallback relay. These requests do not include children’s recordings or works, or KikiDub-created user or device tracking identifiers. Service providers may process connection information such as IP addresses, request times and file paths for delivery and security.

3. 购买及主动售后 / Purchases and optional support

主题购买及恢复由Apple的购买系统处理。KikiDub在设备上读取购买权益以解锁内容，不接收银行卡资料。家长主动发送售后邮件时，我们会收到发件邮箱及邮件内容。可选购买诊断报告包含应用版本、设备型号、系统版本、同步结果、主题权益状态和交易ID；家长可以在邮件发送前查看和编辑。应用不会自动向我们发送该报告。请勿发送不必要的儿童姓名、照片或录音。

Theme purchases and restoration use Apple’s purchase system. KikiDub reads purchase entitlements on the device to unlock content and does not receive payment card details. If a parent sends a support email, we receive the sender’s email address and message. An optional purchase diagnostic report includes app version, device model, OS version, synchronization results, theme entitlements and transaction IDs. Parents can review and edit the email before sending; the app does not automatically send the report. Please avoid sending unnecessary children’s names, photos or recordings.

4. 权限与家庭选择 / Permissions and family choices

拒绝麦克风权限后仍可观看动画，但无法录制配音。相册添加权限仅用于主动保存作品视频，KikiDub不会因此读取整个相册。家长可以通过系统设置管理权限，并在应用中管理本机作品。已导出的文件需要在对应保存位置另行管理。

Without microphone permission, animations remain available but dubbing cannot be recorded. Photo-library add permission is used to save exported work videos, not to read the whole photo library. Parents can manage permissions in system settings and manage local works in the app. Exported copies must be managed separately at their destinations.

5. 儿童与信息用途 / Children and purposes

KikiDub面向儿童，售后与购买事务由家长处理。我们不会将孩子的录音用于广告或训练模型。我们使用家长主动提供的售后资料处理其请求及核对购买问题，不将其用于广告跟踪。安全措施不能消除所有风险，家长应妥善保管设备和主动导出的文件。

KikiDub is designed for children, with purchases and support handled by parents. We do not use children’s recordings for advertising or model training. Information voluntarily provided for support is used to respond to requests and investigate purchase issues, not for advertising tracking. Security measures cannot eliminate all risks; parents should safeguard their devices and exported files.

6. 文件管理、删除与联系 / File management, deletion and contact

本机文件由家庭管理。对已发送给我们的售后资料，家长可发送邮件至qorvia@foxmail.com提出访问、更正或删除请求；处理时可能需要核实请求人与相关邮件或购买的关系。

Local files remain under the family’s control. Parents may contact qorvia@foxmail.com to request access, correction or deletion of support information sent to us. We may need to verify the requester’s relationship to the relevant correspondence or purchase.

7. 政策更新 / Updates

政策变更将在本页面标注日期。需要另行告知或取得同意的变更，应按适用要求处理，不以继续使用替代必要的明确同意。

Policy changes will be dated on this page. Where a change requires notice or consent, applicable requirements must be followed; continued use does not replace any required affirmative consent.
